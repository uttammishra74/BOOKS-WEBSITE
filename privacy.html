Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Freebookleaf does not collect, store, or share any personal information. We use the Google Books API to search for books, which may collect anonymous usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Google Books API

This website uses the Google Books API to provide book search and preview functionality. When you search for a book, your search query is sent to Google. Google's privacy policy applies to this data collection. You can read Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Local Storage

Freebookleaf stores your reading list locally in your browser's storage. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third party.

4. Cookies

We do not use cookies. The Google Books API may use cookies as described in their privacy policy.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no data to request, modify, or delete. You can clear your reading list at any time using the "Clear" button in the reading list panel.
